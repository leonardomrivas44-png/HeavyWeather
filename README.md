# Heavy Weather
A top-down dungeon crawler made in GM2.

### Premise
You play as the weather-wizard JayKay, clearing the mysterious tower of its dangers and growing more powerful along the way.

### Features
* **Randomly generated dungeon layouts:** Featuring checkpoint systems and a final boss.
* **Perk systems:** Increasing stats and unlocking new attacks.
* **Item systems:** Opening playstyles and covering weaknesses of the wizard.
* **Trader:** Buying, selling, or trading weapons or stat increases.
* **Semi-random enemy formats:** Enemy layouts grow difficult as you scale the tower.

### Controls
* `WASD`: Movement (mouse dependent).
* `Shift`: Sprint.
* `Mouse 1` & `Mouse 2`: Attacks (different attacks).
* `1`: Charge attack.
* `I`: Open perk menu.
* `Space`: Use item.
* `Double space`: Use item's secondary ability.
