Heavy Weather
  A top-down dungeon crawler made in GM2

Premise
  You play as the weather-wizard JayKay, clearing the mysterious tower of it's dangers and growing more powerful along the way.

Features
  Randomly generated dungeon layouts: featuring checkpoint systems and a final boss.
  Perk systems: increasing stats and unlocking new attacks.
  Item systems: opening playstyles and covering weaknesses of the wizard.
  Trader: buying, selling, or trading weapons or stat increases.
  Semi-random enemy formats: Enemy layouts grow difficult as you scale the tower.

Controls
  WASD for movement (mouse dependent).
  Shift to sprint.
  Mouse 1 and Mouse 2 for attacks (different attacks).
  1 to charge attack.
  I to open perk menu.
  Space to use item.
  Double space to use item's secondary ability.

Development
  Made and designed in Game-Maker Studio.
  Development began in June 2026 and was concluded exactly September 15th, 2026.
